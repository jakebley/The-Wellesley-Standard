Ask someone why they moved to Wellesley and you'll get the expected answer: the schools, the commute, the trees. Ask them why they stayed, and the answer usually gets more specific — the Saturday morning line at the coffee shop, a particular bench at the reservoir, the way Linden Street looks in the third week of October.

This is the thing a real estate listing can't capture and a ranking can't measure: the texture of actually living somewhere. That's the gap 1881 exists to fill — not another list of "best towns," but an ongoing, honest account of what it's actually like here, week to week.

More on this soon — this is the first entry in what will be a running series on the small stuff that adds up to a town.
